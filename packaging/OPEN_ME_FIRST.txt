Installing Gameline
===================

1. Drag Gameline into the Applications folder.
2. Open Applications and double-click Gameline.

macOS will say Gameline "can't be opened" or "is from an unidentified developer",
because this hackathon build isn't signed with an Apple Developer ID. To open it:

3. Click Done (or OK) on that message.
4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway"
   next to the message about Gameline. Confirm with your password.

You only need to do this once. If macOS instead says Gameline "is damaged",
open Terminal and run:

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Gameline.app

then open Gameline again.

Gameline needs an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) running macOS 13 or newer.
On first launch it walks you through setup; paste a Gemini or Claude API key
when it asks which AI should plan your deadlines.
